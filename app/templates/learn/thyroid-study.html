The data

Prospective study from Padova University Hospital: {{ td.n }} adults scheduled for thyroidectomy, assessed at the anaesthesia visit with almost all the El-Ganzouri items. Cormack 3–4 with a conventional curved blade: {{ td.events }} patients ({{ "%.1f"|format(td.prevalence * 100) }}%). The authors published the data together with the article, as the journal requires: that is why we can use them.

By protocol, patients with an expected difficult intubation were excluded. So the most obvious cases are missing: the predictors look less useful than they would be in practice, and the frequency of Cormack 3–4 is lower. It is the main problem of this study (see PROBAST).

The protocol, written first

Primary model: penalized logistic regression on 7 predictors ({{ model.features|map("feature_label")|join(", ") }}). Clinical reference: the El-Ganzouri ≥ 4 rule as it is. Everything was fixed in SPEC §11 before looking at the results.
